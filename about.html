Our story

Your favourite corner of town

Little Wren opened on Fore Street in 2019 in a former bookshop. We kept the old shelves, added a coffee machine and filled the window with cakes.

We buy our coffee from a roastery just down the road, our eggs from a farm outside town and our bread from the bakery round the corner. Everything else we make ourselves.

Pop in for a takeaway on the way to work, or settle in by the window with the papers. Dogs get a biscuit and a bowl of water.
